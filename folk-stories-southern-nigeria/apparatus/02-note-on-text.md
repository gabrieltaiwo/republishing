# Note on the Text

## Source Edition
The base text for this edition is the 1910 first edition of *Folk Stories from Southern Nigeria, West Africa* by Elphinstone Dayrell, with an introduction by Andrew Lang, published in London by Longmans, Green and Co. The text has been checked against scans preserved in the Harvard University Library and the Project Gutenberg transcription collection.

## Editorial Policy
1. **Preservation of Storytelling Voice**: Dayrell transcribed these stories in direct, rhythmic English as narrated by his Nigerian informants. The original conversational prose and sentence structures have been carefully preserved.
2. **Cultural Terminology and Historical Currency**: Specific regional terms for indigenous items—such as *manillas*, brass rods, and *ju-ju* shrines—have been retained as originally published and defined in the accompanying glossary.
3. **Format and Corrections**: Printer errors, running page numbers, and archaic typographical spacing from the 1910 volume have been corrected.
