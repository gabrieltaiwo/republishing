# Note on the Text

## Source Edition
This edition is based on the 1894 first edition of *The Yoruba-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa: Their Religion, Manners, Customs, Laws, Language, etc.* by A. B. Ellis, published in London by Chapman and Hall, Ltd. The text has been checked against the archival digital scans preserved in the British Library and Project Gutenberg collections.

## Editorial Policy
1. **Victorian Orthography and Terminology**: Ellis utilized an idiosyncratic nineteenth-century phonetic transliteration for Yoruba names and deities (e.g., *Olorun*, *Obatala*, *Shango*, *Orisha*). These original spellings have been preserved in the main text to maintain historical authenticity, while standardized modern spellings are cross-referenced in the glossary.
2. **Anthropological Language**: Nineteenth-century colonial anthropology employed terminology that modern readers may find dated or problematic. In keeping with scholarly standards for historical primary sources, the text has not been expurgated; rather, historical context is provided in the editorial introduction.
3. **Format and Corrections**: Typographical printer errors and broken line hyphenations from the 1894 volume have been silently corrected.
