# Rights file — The History of the Yorubas

- Author: Samuel Johnson (1846–1901), Anglican priest and historian.
- Posthumous editor/compiler: Obadiah Johnson (1849–1920), his brother — recompiled
  the manuscript (lost by the original publisher) from Samuel's notes after Samuel's
  death, then died himself in 1920, months before the book was published.
- Author death year: 1901 (Samuel Johnson, primary author)
- Editor death year: 1920 (Obadiah Johnson)
- First publication year: 1921 — published near-simultaneously in two editions:
  London (George Routledge & Sons) and Lagos (C.M.S. (Nigeria) Bookshops).
