# Glossary of Yoruba Terms, Titles, and Concepts

This glossary provides contextual definitions for classical political offices, religious concepts, and social institutions appearing throughout *The History of the Yorubas*.

### Political and Chieftaincy Titles
- **Alaafin**: The sacred king of Oyo; supreme sovereign of the Yoruba kingdom.
- **Oyo Mesi**: The supreme aristocratic council of seven kingmakers in Oyo, headed by the Bashorun, exercising constitutional checks over the Alaafin.
- **Bashorun**: Prime Minister of Oyo and head of the Oyo Mesi council; commander of the state religion and civic ceremonies.
- **Kakanfo (Aare Ona Kakanfo)**: Supreme Field Marshal of the Yoruba imperial armies. By constitutional tradition, the Kakanfo could never suffer defeat—he was expected either to conquer or commit suicide.
- **Balogun**: Commander-in-chief of provincial armies or city-state forces (particularly prominent in nineteenth-century Ibadan and Abeokuta).
- **Ooni**: The sacred monarch of Ile-Ife, regarded as the spiritual ancestral cradle of all Yoruba lineages.
- **Baale**: The civil headman, mayor, or hereditary governor of an un-crowned town or settlement.
- **Otun / Osi**: Traditional military and civil divisions: *Otun* (Right Wing) and *Osi* (Left Wing).
- **Ilari**: Royal messengers, bodyguards, and diplomatic envoys bearing distinct shaved hairstyles and scarification.

### Cosmology and Religious Concepts
- **Olodumare / Olorun**: The Supreme Deity; Creator of the cosmos and source of *Ase* (spiritual life-force).
- **Orisha**: Divinities and deified ancestral forces representing facets of the cosmic order.
- **Oduduwa**: The mythological progenitor and founding ancestor of the Yoruba royal dynasties.
- **Oranyan**: Son/grandson of Oduduwa; legendary warrior-king and founder of the imperial capital of Oyo-Ile.
- **Sango (Shango)**: The fourth Alaafin of Oyo, renowned for his fiery temper and martial prowess, later deified as the Orisha of thunder, lightning, and justice.
- **Ogun**: The Orisha of iron, metallurgy, craftsmanship, and warfare; guardian of oaths and truth.
- **Obatala (Orisanla)**: The arch-divinity associated with creation, purity, wisdom, and justice.
- **Ifa (Orunmila)**: The divine system of divination, esoteric wisdom, and destiny, mediated through sacred cowrie shells or palm nuts (*Ikin*).
- **Babalawo**: "Father of secrets"; a fully initiated priest of the Ifa divination system.
- **Opon Ifa**: The sacred carved wooden tray utilized by Ifa priests during divination consultations.
- **Egungun**: Masqueraders representing ancestral spirits returning to advise, bless, or discipline the community.
- **Oro**: A sacred male ancestral society utilizing bull-roarers to maintain public order and civic sanctions.

### Geographical and Cultural Terms
- **Aso Oke**: Traditional hand-woven prestige textile cloth woven on narrow looms.
- **Katunga (Oyo-Ile)**: The ancient capital of the Oyo Empire, abandoned during the nineteenth-century conflicts.
- **Ekitiparapo**: The grand confederacy formed by eastern Yoruba kingdoms (Ekiti, Ijesha) to resist Ibadan’s military hegemony during the Kiriji War (1877–1893).
