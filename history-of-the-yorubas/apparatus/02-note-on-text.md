# Note on the Text

## Source Edition
The base text for this edition is the 1921 first edition of *The History of the Yorubas: From the Earliest Times to the Beginning of the British Protectorate*, edited by Dr. Obadiah Johnson and published in London by George Routledge & Sons, Ltd. Digitized scans preserved in the Boston Public Library collection were used as the primary historical baseline.

## Editorial Principles
1. **Preservation of Authorial Voice**: Samuel Johnson’s nineteenth-century English syntax, historical spellings of place names, and archaic missionary terminology have been preserved to maintain the historical integrity of the primary source.
2. **Orthography and Diacritics**: Early missionary orthography for the Yoruba language frequently utilized sub-dots and accent markers. Where early twentieth-century typesetting technology dropped or obscured accents, we have maintained the base phonetic spelling while clarifying terms in the accompanying glossary.
3. **Typography and Layout**: Mechanical scanning artefacts—such as broken line hyphenations, catchwords, and repeated running headers—have been systematically corrected to provide a seamless reading experience on modern digital screens and printed pages.
4. **Footnotes**: All original explanatory footnotes provided by Samuel Johnson and Dr. Obadiah Johnson have been retained and numbered sequentially.
